A quiet harbour. A boat of your own.

Start with a handline on a Norwegian harbour dock and grow your own fishing fleet. Discover 55 species, follow harbour rumours, set nets and sail home under lantern light. A cozy fishing game with a business to build, at your own pace.

Fish from the dock or take your boat out to sea. Read depth and fish activity on the echo sounder, explore island paths and hidden ponds, and follow rumours towards your next species. Hire skippers and invest in your harbour as your business grows.
